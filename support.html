Downloading a language

Mnoria transcribes on your iPhone, so each language is downloaded once. In Mnoria, go to Settings, then Transcription Language, choose your language and tap Download. Recordings made before the download finishes are transcribed as soon as it’s ready.

Summaries and Ask Mnoria

Summaries, automatic titles and answers to your questions are written by Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. They need an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with it turned on in the Settings app under Apple Intelligence & Siri. Recording and transcription work on every supported iPhone.

Taking notes

While recording, tap Note to open a notepad and write what matters to you. Each note is saved at the moment you started writing it, and the recording carries on. Afterwards, the summary starts with Your Notes, each with the details from that moment, and the Notes tab lets you edit them or add more. If you change your notes, tap Rewrite Summary with Your Notes.

Dates mentioned in a recording

When a recording mentions an event or deadline with a specific day, like “the dentist on the 14th at 3:30” or “the copy is due Friday”, its summary lists it under Mentioned Dates. Tap Add to review it in Apple’s event screen and add it to your calendar. To use Fantastical or Google Calendar instead, choose it in Settings › Calendar & Maps › Add With. Google Calendar opens its add-event page in Safari, filled in for you to save. If you’ve never opened Fantastical on your iPhone, the first date may open without its details while the app sets itself up; tap Add again and it’ll be filled in. Tap the quote to hear that moment. To remove a date you don’t need, swipe it to the left or touch and hold it; it stays removed even if you rewrite the summary. Dates you’ve already added to your calendar can’t be removed this way, and dates that have passed disappear by themselves.

Addresses mentioned in a recording

When a recording mentions a street address, like “the apartment at 412 Maple Street” or “22 King Street, Alexandria”, its summary lists it under Mentioned Addresses. Tap Maps to open it in Apple Maps, or in Google Maps or Waze if you choose one in Settings › Calendar & Maps › Open In. If no city was said, the map looks near where you are. If you’ve never opened Google Maps or Waze on your iPhone, the first address may open without its details while the app sets itself up; tap Maps again and it’ll go straight to the address. Tap the quote to hear that moment. Only addresses with a house number are listed, so a passing “on Elm Street” isn’t. To remove an address you don’t need, swipe it to the left or touch and hold it.

Using your calendar events

Use Calendar Events lets Mnoria use your calendar to enhance your recordings. Turn it on in Mnoria’s Settings › Calendar & Maps. If iPhone asks, allow full access to calendars.

Mnoria reads all your calendars, or only the one you choose under Read From. Google and Outlook calendars are included when their account is added in the iPhone’s Settings app, under Calendar › Accounts.

When you record during a meeting that’s in your calendar, Mnoria uses that meeting in three ways:

  • The title. The recording is named after the meeting. Record during “Team Sync” and the recording is called Team Sync.
  • People’s names. The summary uses the names of the people invited, or written in the meeting’s notes, so they’re spelled correctly.
  • The agenda. If the meeting has notes, such as an agenda, the summary uses them to understand what the conversation was about.

Mnoria only looks at the meeting happening while you record. It never changes your calendar, and it all happens on your iPhone. All-day events, like a holiday, are ignored. To turn calendar access off later, open the Settings app, then Privacy & Security, then Calendars.

A recording in another language

If Mnoria notices that a recording doesn’t sound like the language you chose, it says so on the recording. Tap Choose Language to transcribe it again in the right language, and the summary is rewritten too. While recording, you can also change the language straight away.

Recording on Apple Watch

Recordings made on Apple Watch are sent to your iPhone automatically, even if it’s out of range at the time. Keep Mnoria installed on your iPhone, and open it there to transcribe and summarize new recordings. The Watch shows each recording’s progress until it’s ready.

Finding a recording from iPhone search

Swipe down on the Home Screen and type a few words from a recording’s title, summary or what was said. Tap the result to open it in Mnoria. To turn this off, open the Settings app, tap Search, then Mnoria.

Sharing audio and video to Mnoria

In another app, open an audio or video file, tap Share, then tap Mnoria. The first time, you may need to tap More to find Mnoria in the list. The file is added the next time you open Mnoria, then transcribed and summarized like any other recording; from a video, only the sound is kept.

Mnoria appears whenever an app shares an actual audio or video file, for example:

  • Voice Memos: any recording.
  • Files and iCloud Drive: audio and video files.
  • Photos: videos.
  • Mail, Gmail and Outlook: audio and video attachments.
  • Messages: videos and audio files sent to you.
  • Notes: audio recordings and videos in a note.
  • GarageBand and iMovie: songs and videos you share or export.
  • Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive and Box: audio and video files stored there.
  • Telegram and Signal: voice messages and videos.
  • Slack: audio and video files.
  • WhatsApp: videos. Whether voice messages can be shared depends on your version of WhatsApp.

Apps such as Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X and Facebook share a link instead of the file, so Mnoria doesn’t appear there. Music and podcast apps only share links too. If the app lets you save a video to Photos, save it first, then in Mnoria tap + and choose Photos.

Exporting

Open a recording, tap the menu, then Export. Choose what to include: the summary, the transcript, your notes, or all of them together, as PDF, Word, rich text, Markdown or plain text. You can also export the original audio. Export opens on whichever tab you were looking at, and only offers what the recording has.

Subscription and free trial

Mnoria starts with a free week. If you don’t cancel before the week ends, your subscription begins and renews automatically. To cancel or change your plan, open Mnoria’s Settings, tap Subscription, then Manage Subscription. You can also do this in the Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions. On a new iPhone, or after reinstalling, tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen.

If your subscription ends, your recordings stay on your iPhone. You can still open, play and export them. Recording, importing, summaries and Ask Mnoria come back when you subscribe again.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your recordings are part of your iPhone’s backup. Restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, and your recordings come with it.

Privacy

Your recordings stay private, right on your iPhone. Read the full Privacy Policy.
