Support

Mnoria for iPhone and Apple Watch.

Questions, problems or ideas: gaudino.software@gmail.com.

Downloading a language

Mnoria transcribes on your iPhone, so each language is downloaded once. In Mnoria, go to Settings, then Transcription Language, choose your language and tap Download. Recordings made before the download finishes are transcribed as soon as it’s ready.

Summaries and Ask Mnoria

Summaries, automatic titles and answers to your questions are written by Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. They need an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with it turned on in the Settings app under Apple Intelligence & Siri. Recording and transcription work on every supported iPhone.

A recording in another language

If Mnoria notices that a recording doesn’t sound like the language you chose, it says so on the recording. Tap Choose Language to transcribe it again in the right language, and the summary is rewritten too. While recording, you can also change the language straight away.

Recording on Apple Watch

Recordings made on Apple Watch are sent to your iPhone automatically, even if it’s out of range at the time. Keep Mnoria installed on your iPhone, and open it there to transcribe and summarize new recordings. The Watch shows each recording’s progress until it’s ready.

Exporting

Open a recording, tap the menu, then Export. You can export the summary, the transcript or both as PDF, Word, rich text, Markdown or plain text, or export the original audio.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your recordings are part of your iPhone’s backup. Restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, and your recordings come with it.

Privacy

Mnoria never sends your recordings anywhere. Read the full Privacy Policy.
